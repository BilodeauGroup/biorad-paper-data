Figure S11. Surface and bound-protein density profiles

Each CSV contains 400 surface-normal bins. z is in angstroms and density is in heavy
atoms per cubic angstrom. The full arrays are retained; the figure displays z = 2 to
80 angstrom.

Surface density is evaluated within a circle of radius 20 angstrom and smoothed with
a five-bin moving average. Protein density uses the full box cross-section and is
not smoothed. Their different normalization areas must be retained when reproducing
the profiles. The original calculation starts at frame 1000 for Nuvia cPrime and
frame 1250 for CB434 and CB442. Circle centres (x, y), in angstroms, are Nuvia
cPrime (34, 1), CB434 (23, 10), and CB442 (60, 11), with periodic boundaries
applied.
