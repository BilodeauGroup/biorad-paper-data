Figure S7. Ligand-free volume

Each ligand CSV contains the ligand-free volume for cylinder radii of 6, 8, 12, and
20 angstrom. Volumes are in cubic nanometres; radii are in angstroms. The cylinder
height is 14 angstrom. Cubic grid cells have spacing 0.25 angstrom, and a cell is
counted as ligand-free when its centre is more than 4 angstrom from the nearest
ligand heavy atom.

Plotting

From the repository root, run:
  python scripts/plot_ligand_free_probe_volume.py

PDF, SVG, and PNG files are saved in plots/ligand_free_probe_volume/.
