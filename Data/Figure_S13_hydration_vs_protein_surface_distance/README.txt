Figure S13. Hydration of chemical groups during protein adsorption

One CSV per ligand contains the COM distance in nanometres and the mean number of
water molecules around each chemical-group selection. Counts are unnormalized and
are neither smoothed nor divided by the number of groups. Each row is one sampled
umbrella-window distance. No uncertainty band is shown in this figure.

For CB434, phenyl_1 and phenyl_2 identify the two distinct phenyl selections in the
original analysis; they are not replicate measurements. Hydroxyl denotes the
hydroxyl-terminated surface regions. Panels a-c: Nuvia cPrime, CB434, and CB442.
